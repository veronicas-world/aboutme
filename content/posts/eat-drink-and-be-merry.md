This is a list of my favorite restaurants in NYC for various occasions and degrees of formality. They are in no particular order. Do reach out with suggestions, feedback, or best of all, disagreement!

## [Fish Cheeks](https://www.fishcheeksnyc.com/)

This is my favorite Thai restaurant in the city. I (controversially) consider it much better than [Soothr](https://www.soothrnyc.com/) and somewhat better than [Hungry Thirsty](https://www.hungrythirstynyc.com/). I have yet to try its sister restaurant, [Bangkok Supper Club](https://www.bangkoksupperclubnyc.com/), but have heard wonderful things about it.

Standout dishes include steamed fish with Thai herbs, Rawaaeng duck curry, and sautéed cabbage as a side (trust me!!!).

[![Steamed fish, duck curry, and sautéed cabbage at Fish Cheeks](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/fish-cheeks-1.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/fish-cheeks-1.webp)

## [Lucali](https://www.lucali.com/location/lucali-brooklyn/)

This cozy Italian restaurant holds a special place in my heart as it was a frequent destination for me and my close friends in the fall of ’25. It is in the heart of Carroll Gardens, is walk-in only (good luck), and remains the best pizza I have had in New York (my order is a simple margherita with shallots; don’t overdo it, the real gem is the sauce). They also have a (as in, only one) wonderful pasta.

[![A margherita pizza at Lucali](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/lucali-1.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/lucali-1.webp)
[![Two plates of pasta at Lucali](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/lucali-2.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/lucali-2.webp)

## [Umeko](https://www.umekony.com/)

I will admit I have only been here once, which makes it a slightly less defensible pick for this list. That said, this meal was revolutionary. The prix fixe sets are unreal (in both price and taste), and I think they have come out with more options since I went last year. My suggestion – get the toro roll on the side…

[![The sushi set at Umeko, served in a wooden box](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/umeko-1.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/umeko-1.webp)
[![Toro roll on a ceramic plate at Umeko](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/umeko-2.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/umeko-2.webp)

## [The Waverly Inn](https://www.waverlynyc.com/)

Best table bread in the city. By FAR. The ambiance is classic, old-school West Village with an unassuming entrance. Desserts are over the top and so fabulous.

[![A basket of bread and a dish of butter at The Waverly Inn](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/waverly-inn-1.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/waverly-inn-1.webp)
[![A dessert being flambéed at The Waverly Inn](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/waverly-inn-2.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/waverly-inn-2.webp)

## [Hamburger America](https://www.hamburgeramerica.com/)

Best burger in the city. Fight me on this! You will have a hard time doing so; [George Motz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Motz) (head chef) is the preeminent scholar on hamburger history.

[![Burgers and fries on trays at Hamburger America](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/hamburger-america-1.webp)](/writings/eat-drink-and-be-merry/hamburger-america-1.webp)

## [Tacos Cano Taqueria de Guerrero](https://taqueriayfondany.com/)

Whoever said NYC doesn’t have good Mexican has never ventured above 96th Street. [Tacos Cano](https://taqueriayfondany.com/) is a wonderful family-owned restaurant serving up the best quesabirria I have ever had. Close to Columbia and open late.

## [4 Charles](https://www.nycprimerib.com/)

Sorry. It’s really so good. I think the burger is a bit overrated (and you must, must split with 2-4 people), but everything else is out of this world – the French dip, salad, corn, carbonara, etc., etc., etc. I also think their sundae is the BEST dessert in the city (ask for extra cherries on the side), and, of course, the service is outstanding :)
